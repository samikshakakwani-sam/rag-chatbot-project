Sources & Compliance

FundFacts AI answers only from a fixed, curated corpus of official HDFC fund data sourced from Groww. Every reply is grounded in that data and carries a source link — no third-party blogs, no opinions, no predictions.

verified

Our commitment to facts

Responses are generated only from the Curated Corpus — structured snapshots of official HDFC fund pages. A hybrid retrieval engine (keyword + BGE-small semantic search over ChromaDB) selects the relevant facts, and the model is constrained to answer from that context, in ≤3 sentences, with exactly one citation. Advisory, comparison, and return-prediction questions are refused by design.

security

Zero-data privacy notice

Your privacy is non-negotiable. FundFacts AI never asks for or stores PAN, Aadhaar, bank account numbers, OTPs, email, or phone numbers. Inputs are screened for such details and rejected before any processing, and raw input containing PII is never logged. Conversations are stateless and anonymous by design.
